ASBL · 2024-present

RE·ANALYZE · Market Intelligence

43,022 Hyderabad projects, one clean map

Growth needed a single view of supply, price, and demand. The data lived in seven places and disagreed with itself.

Role: data + product Stack: Python, Flask, MongoDB, Leaflet, ECharts, Gemini

  • 43,022 projects in one dataset
  • 7 sources, source-priority rules
  • 10,949 records amenity-enriched

Problem

Hyderabad supply was split across listings, internal files, and scraped feeds. The same tower showed up three times with three prices. Analysts could not answer “what is selling in this locality, in this radius, at this budget?” without a week of spreadsheet work.

Approach

I built a pipeline that merges the seven sources with source-priority rules and fuzzy deduplication at ≥0.85 similarity, then quality checks. Amenities for 10,949 projects were enriched with Gemini Flash. A small Flask app reads MongoDB and draws Leaflet maps plus ECharts panels: pick a locality or a radius, see inventory, pricing, infrastructure, and buyer demand.

A 500-person launch survey sat on the same stack: 61% preferred 3 BHK, median budget ₹1.71 Cr, gated-community demand up from 25% to 43%. That became three buyer personas, not a slide of charts.

Result

One dataset. One map. Analysts stopped reconciling files and started asking market questions. The product is seven files, no separate frontend build, so it stays shippable.
