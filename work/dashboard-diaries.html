ASBL · 2024-present

Dashboard Diaries · Metabase dashboards

10+ hours a week back for 20 stakeholders

Monday used to start with a scrape of spreadsheets. It now starts with a dashboard that already refreshed.

Role: analytics + reporting Stack: Metabase, AWS, CI/CD, monitoring

  • 12+ Metabase dashboards
  • 20+ stakeholders
  • 10+ hours saved each week

Problem

Lead funnel, marketing, sales, and customer KPIs lived in separate pulls. The same questions: where are leads stuck, which stage is leaking, meant a person and a Monday morning.

Approach

I built 12+ Metabase dashboards as one reporting layer. Two of the packs people open most: CRM Dashboard and Lead analysis by current stage. The same production move put ML pipelines on AWS with versioning and daily scoring: refresh time from two days to under an hour.

Result

Twenty-plus stakeholders share one view. Manual reporting dropped more than ten hours a week. The dashboards are the habit; the models are the feed.
