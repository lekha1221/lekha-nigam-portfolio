KALBHOJADITYA · Prospect Intelligence

Top-tier prospects convert at 3.2× the average

After a site visit, every lead looked equally urgent. They were not. Sales needed a daily ranked list, not a gut feel.

Role: modeling + MLOps Stack: XGBoost, Python, AWS, time-aware validation

  • 0.84 ROC-AUC, no leakage
  • 3.2× conversion in tier 1
  • 60+ behavioral features

Problem

Twenty thousand post-site-visit prospects across six projects sat in a CRM with call logs, WhatsApp threads, and visit recency. Follow-up order was whoever shouted last. High-intent buyers went cold while low-intent ones got the next call.

Approach

I built 60+ features from a 1.2M-lead CRM: funnel stage, call activity, visit recency, WhatsApp engagement, website activity. Labels were locked to later time periods so the model could not peek at the future. XGBoost ranked prospects into four intent tiers. A second model covers 200K+ early-stage leads.

The pipeline moved to AWS with versioning, CI/CD, daily scoring, and monitoring. Refresh time dropped from two days to under an hour. The voice-agent backend moved to ECS Fargate in the same pass.

Result

0.84 ROC-AUC on a clean holdout. The top tier converts at 3.2× the average. The model has scored every day since August 2025: sales opens a list, not a debate.
