SAMVAAD · CRM Email Intelligence

70% less time reading the CRM inbox

Customer email was a queue. The same twelve problems hid in 15,000 threads. Nobody could see the pattern without reading every one.

Role: NLP / LLM pipeline Stack: Python, LLMs, text classification, extraction

  • 15,000+ emails classified
  • 90% issue + status accuracy
  • 70% less manual review

Problem

Service and sales shared one inbox. Payment issues sat next to site-visit follow-ups. Review meant a person and a full day. Leadership asked which issues were recurring. The answer was “we would have to read them.”

Approach

I built an LLM pipeline that tags each mail by issue type and resolution status. Prompts were tightened against a labeled sample until accuracy held at 90%. The output is a dashboard slice, not a dump of model text: counts, open vs closed, and the twelve issues that keep coming back.

Result

Twelve recurring issues, named. Manual review dropped 70%. The inbox became a signal: which product or process to fix this week, instead of a pile.
