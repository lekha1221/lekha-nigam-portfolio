EcoGaze · Climate Change Analysis

0.9+ correlation between CO₂ and temperature

A sustainability internship that had to turn 15 years of climate series into a report someone would run again next quarter.

Role: data analyst intern Stack: Python, Power BI, Excel, regression, time-series

  • 15+ years of CO₂, temperature, sea level
  • 0.9+ CO₂-temperature correlation
  • 30% faster recurring reports

Problem

The raw files were long and uneven: CO₂, global temperature anomaly, sea level. Leadership wanted trend, seasonality, and a pack they could refresh, not a one-off notebook.

Approach

Exploratory and correlation analysis first. Regression and time-series decomposition split the long-run rise from seasonal and short-term noise. Power BI dashboards and Excel models held the recurring sustainability report.

Result

A 0.9+ correlation between CO₂ concentration and temperature anomaly. Reporting time dropped about 30%. The internship deliverable was a repeatable pack, not a slide that aged out.
