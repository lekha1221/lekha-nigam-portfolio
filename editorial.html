RE·ANALYZE

Market Intelligence

43,022 Hyderabad projects. Seven sources. One map people actually open.

A Flask and MongoDB product that folds seven real-estate sources into one Hyderabad dataset: 43,022 projects, fuzzy-deduped at ≥0.85 similarity, then mapped. Amenities on 10,949 records were enriched with Gemini Flash. Analysts pick a locality or a radius and read pricing, inventory, infrastructure, and buyer demand in one sitting.

  • 43,022 projects
  • 7 sources
  • 10,949 enriched

Python · Flask · MongoDB · Leaflet · ECharts · Gemini

How to use the demo: pick a locality, click Analyze, then read the KPIs, map, and BHK mix.

Prospect Intelligence
